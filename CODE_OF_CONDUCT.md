# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Discuss ideas and code on their merits. Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Keep issues and pull requests relevant and avoid unsolicited promotion.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when needed, considering context and severity. To report conduct concerns, request a private contact channel from the maintainer without posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be handled with discretion; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

These expectations apply to this repository and project-managed community spaces.
