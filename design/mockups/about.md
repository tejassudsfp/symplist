# About, open source, and help

Read [overall.md](overall.md) and [themes.md](themes.md) first. Produce high-fidelity mockups for this surface and the states below. Closed-beta rules apply throughout.

## Screen and interaction brief

Design a simple About surface within settings or a small standalone sheet. Show the lowercase symplist wordmark, “The most productive thing is often the most simple,” a brief product sentence, and attribution: Tejas Parthasarathi Sudarshan, tejassuds.com.

Show MIT License and a View license action. Repository and self-hosting links can be represented as configured placeholders until real URLs exist; do not invent a GitHub repository address. Self-hosting is fully open source and does not require a paid Symplist license. Avoid implying external model/infrastructure services are free.

A small Help area may link to configured documentation and explain keyboard navigation. Only list shortcuts used in the prototype and label proposals; do not publish an elaborate shortcut catalogue that has no behavior.

Provide version/build information in a quiet secondary area if available. There should be no sales pitch, pricing table, roadmap carousel, support chat bot, or tracking dashboard. Keep the author attribution elegant and unobtrusive.

Show a license-text modal with scrolling and focus behavior, unavailable external-link placeholder treatment, and desktop/mobile layouts. Quirky themes can use one small mark here, but no forced mascot or oversized illustration. The primary exit is Back to settings/workspace.

## Handoff

Use the frame naming and theme coverage in overall.md. Annotate entry/exit, primary and secondary actions, focus behavior, responsive changes, and persistence expectations. Include meaningful error/loading states rather than only the successful screen. Reuse shared components; do not add unrequested billing or automation features.
